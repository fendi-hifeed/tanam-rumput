# Logo Design Prompt — PT Lampung Tanam Rumput (PLTR)

## Company Identity

**Full name:** PT Lampung Tanam Rumput
**Short name:** PLTR
**Tagline:** "Soil first. People always."
**Industry:** Regenerative agriculture — land restoration, forage cultivation, nursery operations, commercial biomass production
**Location:** Lampung Province, Indonesia

---

## What PLTR Does

PLTR transforms degraded and underutilised land into productive regenerative agricultural infrastructure. Key activities:
- Soil health improvement
- Regenerative forage and biomass cultivation
- Commercial nursery operations
- Rural women's activation as paid workers and suppliers

## Visual Identity

### Color Palette
| Color | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Forest Green | `#2F4A2A` | Primary — growth, regeneration, trust |
| Soil Brown | `#7A4E2D` | Secondary — earth, roots, land |
| Harvest Gold | `#C9A961` | Accent — KPI highlights, warmth |
| Cream | `#F5EFE3` | Background — warmth, natural |
| Ink | `#1B1B17` | Text — authority, readability |

### Typography
- **Display:** Fraunces (serif, editorial, warm)
- **Body:** Inter (clean, legible, modern)

### Aesthetic Direction
Clean, editorial, professional. Think: agricultural trust meets design editorial. Earthy but not rustic. Trustworthy but not corporate. NOT clip-art grass or cartoon plants.

---

## Logo Design Brief

### Concept A — "Rooted Growth"
A geometric mark combining:
- A simplified top-down or side-view of grass/blade forms emerging from soil
- The "PLTR" initials rendered in a modified Fraunces or similar editorial serif, with the first "L" subtly shaped like a young plant or seedling
- Negative space used to suggest roots going into earth
- Single-color mark (Forest Green) or two-color (Forest Green + Soil Brown)

**In use:** Logo sits on cream background. Tagline "Soil first. People always." in Inter below or beside the mark.

### Concept B — "The Cycle"
A circular/loop mark suggesting:
- A closed loop: soil → plant → harvest → return to soil
- Stylized as 2–3 curved lines forming a subtle cycle/circle, evoking both a seed leaf cross-section and a renewal arrow
- "PLTR" wordmark in Inter Bold alongside or below
- Earthy two-tone (Forest Green + Harvest Gold)

### Concept C — "Text-First Editorial"
- "PLTR" in a strong, custom-modified Fraunces with the letters slightly extended/stylized to suggest growth (ascenders that curve like grass blades, or letters that sit firmly like they grow from a baseline)
- Full name "PT Lampung Tanam Rumput" in Inter Light/Semibold below
- Minimalist — the typography IS the logo
- Tagline as supporting copy, not part of the mark

### Concept D — "Soil Cross-Section"
A minimalist linear mark:
- A horizontal line (soil surface) with vertical strokes rising (vegetation) and subtle roots descending below
- Combines landscape cross-section aesthetic with bar-chart readability
- "PLTR" in Inter Bold to the right
- Very clean, almost topographic-map feel

---

## What the Logo Must Communicate

**Must convey:**
- Land restoration / soil health
- Agricultural productivity
- Growth (literal and economic)
- Grounded, trustworthy, professional
- Rural warmth (not cold corporate)

**Must NOT convey:**
- Generic "green eco" clip art
- Cartoon plants or leaves
- Overly corporate financial services aesthetic
- Tropical beach/resort feel
- Charity/NGO handout aesthetic

---

## Technical Requirements

- **Format:** SVG (primary), PNG 2x for print
- **Versions needed:**
  - Full logo: Mark + "PLTR" + full company name + tagline
  - Compact: Mark + "PLTR" only (for favicon, app icon)
  - Reversed: White version for dark backgrounds
- **Color variants:** Full color, Forest Green mono, dark/ink mono, reversed
- **Clear space:** Minimum 1x height of "PLTR" around all sides
- **Minimum size:** 16px height for smallest use (favicon)
